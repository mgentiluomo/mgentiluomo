I'm a geneticist at the University of Pisa working on the genetic epidemiology of complex traits, with a focus on pancreatic cancer: who develops it, who survives it and who responds to therapy.

### What you'll find here

- **[how-to-gwas-pdac](https://github.com/mgentiluomo/how-to-gwas-pdac)**: a step-by-step annotated GWAS guide using pancreatic cancer as a case study, developed within the COST Action TRANSPAN (CA21116).
- **[GWAS_training_session](https://github.com/mgentiluomo/GWAS_training_session)**: materials and practical exercises from a GWAS training course.
